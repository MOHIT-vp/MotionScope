# MotionScope

Lucas–Kanade optical flow, motion saliency and vehicle tracking for traffic video, with calibrated speed estimation.

Upload a short traffic clip, optionally calibrate the road plane, and get an annotated video, a motion report, tracks and heat maps.

This hosted demo processes videos up to 50 MB and 60 seconds; uploads and results are deleted after one hour.

Source code: [github.com/MOHIT-vp/MotionScope](https://github.com/MOHIT-vp/MotionScope)
