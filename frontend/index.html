From raw frames to measured tracks.

Seven stages run on every frame. Saliency and Lucas–Kanade tracking are the project's original method, kept with its original parameters; the rest makes the measurements trustworthy.

Original project methodAdded in reconstruction

  1. 01

    Resize and grayscale

    Long side scaled to 640–1280 px. Pixel results are reported back in source-video pixels.

    Original
  2. 02

    Camera-motion estimate

    Background corners tracked between frames; a RANSAC similarity transform removes hand-held shake before anything is measured.

    Added
  3. 03

    Motion saliency

    Pixels that changed by more than τ = 30 are flagged; an exponential average keeps a short memory of where motion happens.

    Bt = 1[ |It − It−1| > τ ]
St = 0.9 · St−1 + 0.1 · Bt
    Original
  4. 04

    Lucas–Kanade feature tracking

    Shi–Tomasi corners inside the saliency mask, followed with a 3-level pyramid and a 15 × 15 window. A forward–backward check discards unreliable points.

    [ ΣIₓ²    ΣIₓI_y ] [u]     [ ΣIₓI_t ]
[ ΣIₓI_y  ΣI_y²  ] [v] = − [ ΣI_yI_t ]
    Original
  5. 05

    Moving-vehicle detection

    YOLOX boxes every second frame, kept only if the box actually moves. Motion blobs are the fallback when the model is not installed.

    Added
  6. 06

    Tracking and measurement

    A SORT-style tracker whose prediction is the median LK flow inside each box, giving persistent IDs, displacement, direction and px/s.

    Added
  7. 07

    Render and report

    H.264 video with the overlays you choose, plus JSON, CSV, a motion heat map and a trajectory map.

    Added

Schematic, not to scale

Pixels per second is not kilometres per hour.

The camera sees a projection. A car covering 10 m moves hundreds of pixels near the lens and a handful near the horizon, so no single metres-per-pixel factor is correct. MotionScope keeps two kinds of numbers apart.

Measured
Displacement, flow in px/s, direction, durations and counts, read straight from the pixels.
Estimate
Speed in km/h. Mark four road points forming a rectangle of known size; a homography maps each vehicle's ground point to metres, and every speed carries its interquartile range.

Accuracy depends on the reference distances, a correct frame rate, a flat road, and the bottom of each box touching the road.
